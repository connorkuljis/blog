Workplace Playbook

  • acknowledge that you are playing a part in the game.

  • imagine the workplace like an being a passenger on an airline flight.

  • as a passenger you must:

    • arrive on time.

    • present physical hygeniene

    • display friendly mannerisms - but don't expect others to do the same.

    • research the destintion and have a booking for when you get off.

      • don't leave without a rough plan if you have responsibilites.

      • if you don't have responsibilites you can yolo it.

    • read the safety instructions when you get on

    • be very attentive at the start, once everyone is tired you can start to move around the plane.

    • flirt with the hostess but don't get caught.

    • don't fight or scream at other passengers.

    • order food and drink when it's expected of you.

    • emergency doors are for emergencies.

    • turbulance is normal.

Never rent your heart and mind out, rent out you hands for the duration of the flight.
