Entropy

In university I went about preparing for my mid-terms, assessments and finals in the least roundabout manner possible.

My standard operating procedure was to find which questions appeared frequently and to only study those.

In other words, to minimise entropy, because there were no incentives for studying something if it did not come up during an assesment.

Now that I have graduated, my guess is that this mode of operation will
continue in the workplace, especially in large corportations, due the incentive structure.

In the workplace, no matter how hard you work, at end of 28 days you still get paid the same amount.

All you can do is embrace the obnoxious meaninglessness of it all.

"They pretended to teach, and I pretended to learn" - @pmarca
