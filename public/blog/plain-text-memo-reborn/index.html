Plain Text Memo: Reborn

Dec 24, 2024

I am very bad at narrative writing, in fact I could not even spell 'narrative' correctly - twice.

When researching Jeff Bezos' writing management strategy at Amazon, I asked myself: Why tell a story to executive members when meetings are all about objectives and metrics?

At Amazon all meetings operate around a six page memo.

I like this idea because it seperates presentation from content (the essential part). If a memo were a website, it would be plain html with default styling.

I think writing is hard because good writing gets to the essence of things.
